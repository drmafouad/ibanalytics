The Method

Research You Can Actually Act On

Every report is crafted with intention — then delivered as a live, interactive dashboard built for decision-making.

1. Deep Research, Done For You

Each stock goes through a rigorous review — price structure, earnings momentum, and market context — so you're always working from a complete picture.

2. Every Signal, In One Place

We distill the most important signals into a clear, consistent format. No noise. No hunting through pages of text. Just what you need to know.

3. A Dashboard Built for Action

Your report becomes a live workspace — charts that move with the market, risk tools you can adjust, and a layout designed to support your trading decisions.

What You Get

Every Report Includes

Live TradingView Charts

Embedded, interactive TradingView charts directly inside each report. Full daily chart with drawing tools, indicators, and symbol switching.

Swing Model Spec Sheet

Precise entry zones, stop-loss levels, and scaling targets with an interactive price ladder. Built-in action checklist with localStorage persistence.

Quantitative Signal Gauges

RSI, ADX, and Relative Strength visualised as animated SVG arc gauges with bullish/neutral/bearish zone indicators. Loads with smooth animation.

Risk Calculator

Interactive position sizing calculator. Input account size and risk %, instantly get share count, dollar risk, and risk-to-reward ratio for the trade.

Institutional Flow Analysis

Institutional bias gauge shows buy/sell pressure. Short interest percentage and trend (rising/declining) displayed with colour-coded signals.

Financial Health Metrics

Forward P/E, PEG ratio, gross margin, and debt-to-equity displayed as clean stat cards alongside the full 6-indicator technical suite table.
